Our process explained

Know About Our
Working Process

Five simple steps from first click to a protected property — no pressure, no surprise pricing.

Browse treatment options, read what's included and get transparent pricing guidance before you speak to anyone. Our AI assistant is on hand 24/7 if you'd rather just ask.

Tell us what you've seen and where. We'll identify the likely species, flag any urgency and book a free on-site inspection at a time that suits you.

After the inspection you get a written plan with a fixed price — one-off treatment or an ongoing protection plan. You choose, and nothing starts until you approve it.

Your technician arrives in a marked vehicle, treats the property, seals entry points where needed and walks you through exactly what was done and what to expect next.

We follow up automatically, monitor for return activity and re-treat free of charge if anything reappears between scheduled visits.
